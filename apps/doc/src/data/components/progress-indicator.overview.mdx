Use ProgressIndicator to show the progress of an operation, either as a
determinate value or as an unspecified wait time.

<Code
  codes={{

    angular: progressIndicatorDeterminateAngularSource,

>
  <ProgressIndicatorDeterminateReact slot="react" client:load />
  <ProgressIndicatorDeterminateAngular slot="angular" client:load />
</Code>

## Variants

- `linear-determinate` / `circular-determinate`: reflect a known `value` (0–100).
- `linear-indeterminate` / `circular-indeterminate`: an unbounded animation; `value` is ignored.

<Code
  codes={{

## Accessibility

Every variant renders `role="progressbar"` with `aria-valuemin`/`aria-valuemax`;
determinate variants also expose `aria-valuenow`. Provide `aria-label` or
`aria-labelledby` — the component does not infer an accessible name.
